# Kriterion Academic — Product Truth & Positioning

## 1. Product Identity
- **Name:** Kriterion Academic
- **Category:** Academic Preflight Suite & Editorial Integrity Assistant
- **Positioning:** The ethical, client-side "Robinhood" for students, thesis writers, researchers, and professors. We stand against predatory $30/mo subscription paywalls and reject shady "AI humanizer spinners".
- **Primary Platform:** Web (modern desktop and responsive mobile browsers).

## 2. Target Audience & Critical Pain Points
- **Primary Users:** University students (undergrad, master's, PhD candidates), thesis writers, academic researchers, and faculty reviewers.
- **Critical Situation:** Preparing manuscripts for official submission, defense, or peer review under severe fear of unfair AI-detector false positives, formatting rejection, or accidental plagiarism flags.
- **Core Problem:** Commercial tools (Turnitin, Copyleaks, GPTZero) are opaque black boxes that hoard user data to train proprietary models, charge exorbitant subscriptions, and produce false positives on human academic prose.

## 3. Core Mechanisms & Guarantees
- **100% Client-Side Processing:** No manuscripts, thesis drafts, or excerpts are ever transmitted to cloud servers. Analysis runs locally in the user's browser.
- **Spatial Word & PDF Parity:** Advanced spatial reconstruction parses text while automatically isolating frontmatter (covers, MBA degrees, tables of contents) so that Word (`.docx`) and PDF inputs yield identical metrics.
- **Empirical Diagnostics:** Instead of inventing a dogmatic "80% AI" percentage, Kriterion provides scientific lexical predictability, burstiness/cadence variance, token entropy, and reproducible cryptographic SHA-256 proofs.
- **Multilingual Support:** Dynamic language detection (`navigator.language`) supporting Spanish (`es`), English (`en`), and Portuguese (`pt`), with instant manual switching.

## 4. Product Voice & Tone
- **Tone:** Empathetic, rigorous, calm, scientifically honest, and sovereign.
- **Anti-Promises:** We never claim to "bypass" or "trick" university detectors. We provide an editorial flight-check to ensure genuine human scholarship shines with zero synthetic noise.
